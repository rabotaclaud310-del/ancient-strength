Ancient Strength Uploader

Ancient Strength Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the Ancient Strength channel. It prepares our own videos (script, narration, subtitles, images) and publishes them to our own YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to other users and does not access anyone else's YouTube account.
