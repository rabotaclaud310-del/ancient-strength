Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ancient Strength Uploader (the "Tool"), operated by the owner of the Ancient Strength YouTube channel ("we", "us"), handles information. The Tool is a private application used only by the channel owner to upload the channel's own videos to YouTube.

1. Use of YouTube API Services

The Tool uses YouTube API Services to upload videos and set their titles, descriptions, tags and privacy status on our own YouTube channel, and to read basic information about that channel (its name and subscriber count) to confirm we are connected to the right channel.

By using the Tool you are agreeing to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

2. Information we access

When the channel owner signs in with Google (OAuth 2.0), the Tool requests only these permissions:

  • youtube.upload — to upload videos to the owner's channel;
  • youtube.readonly — to read the name and basic statistics of the owner's channel.

The Tool does not access, collect or process data of any other YouTube users, viewers or channels. It does not read comments, subscribers' personal data, watch history or private messages.

3. How information is stored

The only data the Tool stores is the OAuth access/refresh token, saved in a local file on the owner's own computer. It is never sent to us, to any server we operate, or to any third party other than Google. Video files and metadata are created locally by the owner and sent only to YouTube.

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent, share or disclose any user data to third parties. Data is exchanged only between the Tool and Google/YouTube as needed to upload videos.

5. Cookies and tracking

The Tool and this website do not use cookies, analytics, advertising identifiers or any other tracking technologies, and do not store information on or read information from users' devices or browsers.

6. Revoking access and deleting data

  • Access can be revoked at any time via the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
  • Revoking access, or deleting the local token file, immediately and permanently removes the Tool's ability to act on the account. No copy of the token or of any YouTube data is kept elsewhere.
  • Any YouTube API data the Tool holds is kept only as long as necessary and is deleted within 30 days at the latest, or immediately on request.

7. Children

The Tool is not directed to children and is used only by the adult owner of the channel.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
